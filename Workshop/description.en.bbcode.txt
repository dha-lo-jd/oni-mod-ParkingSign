[h1]Parking Sign[/h1]

Adds two signs to the [b]Utilities[/b] menu that call chosen Duplicants over to a spot and keep them there: the floor-mounted [b]Parking Sign[/b] and the ceiling-mounted [b]Hanging Parking Sign[/b]. Set each sign's priority with the priority tool, and check the Duplicants you want to assign to it.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] When a sign receives a [b]green automation signal[/b], it calls the checked Duplicants over. Turn the signal off, or uncheck a Duplicant in the sign's settings, to let them move elsewhere
[*] Duplicants head to the [b]highest-priority sign[/b] they can reach. If it becomes unreachable, the next-highest reachable sign takes over
[*] Once they arrive, they wait there and can still perform any work [b]within reach of their position[/b]
[*] The [b]Parking Sign[/b] is placed on a floor. The [b]Hanging Parking Sign[/b] is placed on a ceiling tile (no floor needed) and calls Duplicants to the floor or ladder directly beneath it
[*] Both signs are [b]1x1[/b] and cost [b]25 kg[/b] of [b]Raw Mineral[/b]
[*] Use the game's standard [b]priority tool[/b] to set a sign's priority. Signs with equal priority favor the one built first
[*] Checked Duplicants and priorities are [b]saved[/b] with the building
[*] English and Japanese
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*] A Duplicant that is called to a sign does not start work at a spot it cannot reach: a redirected arrival does not count as arriving at the original task's destination.
[*] If no checked, active sign is reachable, no sign attracts the Duplicant.
[*] When the signal turns red or a Duplicant is unchecked, the current destination is discarded and the game assigns a fresh task as usual.
[*] A Hanging Parking Sign needs a standable tile within 10 tiles below it; otherwise it shows a "No standing tile" status.
[/list]
